FONTLOG for Very Peri Serif
===========================

This file records the history of this modified version of Instrument Serif, as SIL recommends for OFL fonts.

Basic font information
----------------------
Very Peri Serif is a Cyrillic extension of Instrument Serif (Regular and Italic): Russian and Kazakh letters,
Cyrillic OpenType features (contextual forms, stylistic sets, Serbian and Macedonian italic forms) and the
punctuation and currency signs these languages need. The Latin of Instrument Serif is kept unchanged.
Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt). No Reserved Font Name is declared.

What was changed against the original
-------------------------------------
- Added: 84 Cyrillic letters in Regular and Italic, Cyrillic alternates, tenge, ruble, hryvnia and numero signs,
  typographic spaces and controls, Latin schwa.
- Added OpenType: calt, ss01-ss10, locl (Serbian, Macedonian), Cyrillic kerning, a STAT table.
- Renamed: the family is Very Peri Serif; the original name Instrument Serif is not used as a font name.
- Not changed: the native Latin outlines, spacing and kerning of Instrument Serif.
- Regular: the Cyrillic de is a modified outline of Cormorant Garamond (SIL OFL 1.1, the same licence; its
  copyright line opens OFL.txt), taken at the author's explicit request. No other outline of any third-party font
  is included.

How it was made (transparency, Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Artificial Intelligence", art. 21)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Cyrillic outlines were built with the assistance of AI systems (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI Codex) by tools
in this repository, mostly by assembling parts of the native Instrument Serif glyphs and by measured rules.
The human author directed the work and made every design decision: the brief and the instruction
(docs/instruction.md), the choice among variants, rejections and corrections (the decision log in CLAUDE.md
records each one with the author's words), and own drawings (the vertical Ц and the tail motifs, sent as SVG and
embedded without changes). Apart from the Cormorant de stated above, no outline of any other typeface is included.

Change log
----------
Regular: 1.000 (17.10.2026) first release; 1.001-1.016 spacing, kerning, outline polish, straight edges.
Italic: test builds I001-I025 (17-19.10.2026), see data/italic-version.json; not yet a final release.
Family: Regular and Italic linked as one family Very Peri Serif (19.10.2026).

20 October 2026 — Family release 1.100 (Regular and Italic; internal drawing I027).
At the author’s request, Codex independently implements and checks the premium audit:
- Native TrueType outlines, metrics and hint programs for declared Regular homoglyphs.
- Native-compatible mark anchors on constructed Italic bases and alternatives.
- Italic kerning in all case orders, mixed partners, punctuation, digits and currencies.
- Narrower reconstructed Italic zhe; Regular rounded de loop shortened to -250.
- Author-requested ж/з detail repairs: clean ж stem without k branch stubs, tangent-aligned bowl shoulder; з upper shoulder 4.6 degrees to zero (about 1 unit of contour displacement).
- Optional tabular figures; rejected PUA experiments kept only in development sources.
- Independent HarfBuzz/vector checks, offline FontBakery Universal and Hyperglot.
- Complete alphabet/specimen proofs; Linux Chromium checks of all six font formats.
- Versioned immutable download archives. Attribution of the Cormorant-derived de retained.
Native Windows/macOS and commercial application checks are not claimed; see docs/premium-acceptance.md.

21 October 2026 — Family release 1.101 (internal drawing I028).
At the author's request, the default lowercase italic ghe is the exact native body
of the Serbian locl form without its macron (dotlessi, including its hints and metrics).
Serbian/Macedonian locl and the printed ss04 form are retained. Kerning is rebuilt
for the new width; stress marks and every Cyrillic pair/feature mode are checked.
Previous drawing sources and release archives are preserved.

21 October 2026 — Family release 1.102 (internal drawing I029).
Kazakh lowercase italic barred ghe follows the selected default ghe body, with
a merged crossbar at the previous height and weight, matching advance and mark
anchors. The italic de bowl retains the native q round overshoot at -9 instead
of being clipped at the baseline; its descender and advance are retained.
Kerning is rebuilt, with independent outline-profile and shaping regressions.
Final-font comparisons, enlarged proofs and six-format browser/PDF checks are
recorded with the release. Native Latin and previous archives are preserved.

21 October 2026 — Family release 1.103 (internal drawing I030).
The author's enlargement revealed a nine-unit lower hairline in italic ya.
Its native-g bowl clipping ellipse is deepened, bringing the minimum lower
arc to 15 units without changing the stem, leg or advance. Final-font profile,
curve, shaping and package checks accompany the release. Older archives stay.

21 October 2026 — Family release 1.104 (internal drawing I031).
The remaining donor seams of italic ya are smoothed: the top bowl-to-stem
notch and the pointed lower link-to-stem spur now use tangent-matched quadratic
splines. Bowl, counter, advance and side bearings are unchanged. Final-font
outline comparison and enlarged seam proofs accompany the release.

21 October 2026 — Family release 1.105 (internal drawing I031).
Both sharp leg-to-bowl shoulders of Regular lowercase ya are replaced with
compact tangent-matched quadratic curves. Advance, side bearings, bounds,
bowl, terminals and the Italic drawing are unchanged. Final-font curve,
scope, shaping and enlarged comparison checks accompany the release.

21 October 2026 — Family release 1.106 (internal drawing I031).
The remaining kink on the inner left edge of the right foot of Regular
lowercase ya is removed by placing its adjacent quadratic controls on one
exact tangent. Advance, bearings, bounds and the Italic drawing are unchanged.

21 October 2026 — Family release 1.107 (internal drawing I031).
The author confirmed that the 1.106 tangent fix still left a visible wave.
Replace the complete left edge of the right foot of Regular ya with one
convex cubic from the bottom to the straight stem. Validate monotone edge
slope over the whole exported curve instead of testing one collinear node.

21 October 2026 — Family release 1.108 (internal drawing I032).
Audit all 1,016 glyphs, including components, stylistic/local forms and signs.
Reviewed quadratic sources replace accidental waves, micro-steps and uneven
stem/serif joins of constructed glyphs. Preserve all advances, side bearings,
accepted ya forms and inherited native outlines/hinting. Curve sources apply
after provisional generation and before kerning; complete outline/feature,
collision, language, sanitizer and browser checks accompany the release.

21 October 2026 — Family release 1.109 (internal drawing I033).
Author-reported ya defects: the Regular crown had tangent continuity but an
uneven curvature change. Replace its upper-right arc with reviewed quadratics
whose exported crown curvatures agree. Italic ya loses its short stem jog and
exit-cap corner. Preserve counters, all advances/bearings and bounds.
Only lowercase ya changes outline in each style relative to 1.108.
OTF ya uses exact quadratic degree elevation with fractional controls, avoiding
new CFF fitting/rounding defects. Exported cubic controls are independently checked.

22 October 2026 — Family release 1.110 (internal drawing I034).
The author requested a wider default Italic ghe. Extend the entry and exit
flourishes while keeping the diagonal stem's weight and angle; advance 288 to
308. Apply the same width adjustment to barred ghe, shift both mark anchors by 10,
and compute independent Cyrillic kerning. Preserve native Latin dotlessi,
Serbian/Macedonian imacron, the printed alternate, Regular and all other outlines.

25 October 2026 — Family release 1.111 (internal drawing I035).
First contour-audit repair batch: remove confirmed adjacent-quadratic self-crossing loops at the pointed Italic ц, щ and ң tail/body joins. Only two points per glyph change; all other points, glyphs, advances, bearings, native hints, marks, features and kerning are retained. Exact degree elevation preserves these three outlines in CFF. Full contour triage remains open; this is a targeted correction release.

25 October 2026 — Family release 1.112 (internal drawing I036).
Replace the author-reported transverse step in Italic ч by one convex quadratic. Preserve the rest of the glyph, all other glyphs, metrics, marks, ligatures and kerning. Exact CFF degree elevation preserves the repaired arc. Add a transverse-step detector and regression for the reported seam. Full contour triage remains open.

25 October 2026 — Frozen release 1.113 (internal drawing I037).
Losslessly remove 57 redundant stored TrueType points across 38 authored glyphs, without refitting curves. Preserve accepted CFF programs, native outlines/hints, all metrics, marks, kerning and ligatures. Identify the Cyrillic author as Yerbolat Rakhatuly (DEW) in font metadata and accompanying documents. Preserve original Instrument Serif and Cormorant notices and the unmodified SIL OFL 1.1 license body.

2026-10-26 — Version 1.114 / Italic I038
-------------------------------------
Added optional italic lowercase Cyrillic el with a rounded arch and the original
exit stroke, selected by stylistic set ss10 or the alternates menu (aalt).
Drawing selected by Yerbolat Rakhatuly (DEW). The alternate has its own spacing,
kerning and mark anchors. All existing default glyphs, metrics, native hinting
and layout behavior are retained. TTF, OTF and WOFF2 include the feature.

2026-10-27 — Version 1.115 / Italic I039
-------------------------------------
The author-selected arched lowercase Cyrillic el is now the default in Italic.
The previous diagonal el is retained in stylistic set ss10 and aalt.
Both drawings retain their exact outlines, advances, kerning and mark anchors.
Regular and every other default glyph remain unchanged.
