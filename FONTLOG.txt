FONTLOG for Very Peri Serif
===========================

This file records the history of this modified version of Instrument Serif, as SIL recommends for OFL fonts.

Basic font information
----------------------
Very Peri Serif is a Cyrillic extension of Instrument Serif (Regular and Italic): Russian and Kazakh letters,
Cyrillic OpenType features (contextual forms, stylistic sets, Serbian and Macedonian italic forms) and the
punctuation and currency signs these languages need. The Latin of Instrument Serif is kept unchanged.
Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt). No Reserved Font Name is declared.

What was changed against the original
-------------------------------------
- Added: 84 Cyrillic letters in Regular and Italic, Cyrillic alternates, tenge, ruble, hryvnia and numero signs,
  typographic spaces and controls, Latin schwa.
- Added OpenType: calt, ss01-ss09, locl (Serbian, Macedonian), Cyrillic kerning, a STAT table.
- Renamed: the family is Very Peri Serif; the original name Instrument Serif is not used as a font name.
- Not changed: the native Latin outlines, spacing and kerning of Instrument Serif.
- Regular: the Cyrillic de is a modified outline of Cormorant Garamond (SIL OFL 1.1, the same licence; its
  copyright line opens OFL.txt), taken at the author's explicit request. No other outline of any third-party font
  is included.

How it was made (transparency, Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Artificial Intelligence", art. 21)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Cyrillic outlines were built with the assistance of AI systems (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI Codex) by tools
in this repository, mostly by assembling parts of the native Instrument Serif glyphs and by measured rules.
The human author directed the work and made every design decision: the brief and the instruction
(docs/instruction.md), the choice among variants, rejections and corrections (the decision log in CLAUDE.md
records each one with the author's words), and own drawings (the vertical Ц and the tail motifs, sent as SVG and
embedded without changes). Apart from the Cormorant de stated above, no outline of any other typeface is included.

Change log
----------
Regular: 1.000 (17.10.2026) first release; 1.001-1.016 spacing, kerning, outline polish, straight edges.
Italic: test builds I001-I025 (17-19.10.2026), see data/italic-version.json; not yet a final release.
Family: Regular and Italic linked as one family Very Peri Serif (19.10.2026).

20 October 2026 — Family release 1.100 (Regular and Italic; internal drawing I027).
At the author’s request, Codex independently implements and checks the premium audit:
- Native TrueType outlines, metrics and hint programs for declared Regular homoglyphs.
- Native-compatible mark anchors on constructed Italic bases and alternatives.
- Italic kerning in all case orders, mixed partners, punctuation, digits and currencies.
- Narrower reconstructed Italic zhe; Regular rounded de loop shortened to -250.
- Author-requested ж/з detail repairs: clean ж stem without k branch stubs, tangent-aligned bowl shoulder; з upper shoulder 4.6 degrees to zero (about 1 unit of contour displacement).
- Optional tabular figures; rejected PUA experiments kept only in development sources.
- Independent HarfBuzz/vector checks, offline FontBakery Universal and Hyperglot.
- Complete alphabet/specimen proofs; Linux Chromium checks of all six font formats.
- Versioned immutable download archives. Attribution of the Cormorant-derived de retained.
Native Windows/macOS and commercial application checks are not claimed; see docs/premium-acceptance.md.

21 October 2026 — Family release 1.101 (internal drawing I028).
At the author's request, the default lowercase italic ghe is the exact native body
of the Serbian locl form without its macron (dotlessi, including its hints and metrics).
Serbian/Macedonian locl and the printed ss04 form are retained. Kerning is rebuilt
for the new width; stress marks and every Cyrillic pair/feature mode are checked.
Previous drawing sources and release archives are preserved.

21 October 2026 — Family release 1.102 (internal drawing I029).
Kazakh lowercase italic barred ghe follows the selected default ghe body, with
a merged crossbar at the previous height and weight, matching advance and mark
anchors. The italic de bowl retains the native q round overshoot at -9 instead
of being clipped at the baseline; its descender and advance are retained.
Kerning is rebuilt, with independent outline-profile and shaping regressions.
Final-font comparisons, enlarged proofs and six-format browser/PDF checks are
recorded with the release. Native Latin and previous archives are preserved.

21 October 2026 — Family release 1.103 (internal drawing I030).
The author's enlargement revealed a nine-unit lower hairline in italic ya.
Its native-g bowl clipping ellipse is deepened, bringing the minimum lower
arc to 15 units without changing the stem, leg or advance. Final-font profile,
curve, shaping and package checks accompany the release. Older archives stay.
