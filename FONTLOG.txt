FONTLOG for Very Peri Serif
===========================

This file records the history of this modified version of Instrument Serif, as SIL recommends for OFL fonts.

Basic font information
----------------------
Very Peri Serif is a Cyrillic extension of Instrument Serif (Regular and Italic): Russian and Kazakh letters,
Cyrillic OpenType features (contextual forms, stylistic sets, Serbian and Macedonian italic forms) and the
punctuation and currency signs these languages need. The Latin of Instrument Serif is kept unchanged.
Licence: SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt). No Reserved Font Name is declared.

What was changed against the original
-------------------------------------
- Added: 84 Cyrillic letters in Regular and Italic, Cyrillic alternates, tenge, ruble, hryvnia and numero signs,
  typographic spaces and controls, Latin schwa.
- Added OpenType: calt, ss01-ss09, locl (Serbian, Macedonian), Cyrillic kerning, a STAT table.
- Renamed: the family is Very Peri Serif; the original name Instrument Serif is not used as a font name.
- Not changed: the native Latin outlines, spacing and kerning of Instrument Serif.
- Regular: the Cyrillic de is a modified outline of Cormorant Garamond (SIL OFL 1.1, the same licence; its
  copyright line opens OFL.txt), taken at the author's explicit request. No other outline of any third-party font
  is included.

How it was made (transparency, Law of the Republic of Kazakhstan "On Artificial Intelligence", art. 21)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Cyrillic outlines were built with the assistance of AI systems (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI Codex) by tools
in this repository, mostly by assembling parts of the native Instrument Serif glyphs and by measured rules.
The human author directed the work and made every design decision: the brief and the instruction
(docs/instruction.md), the choice among variants, rejections and corrections (the decision log in CLAUDE.md
records each one with the author's words), and own drawings (the vertical Ц and the tail motifs, sent as SVG and
embedded without changes). Apart from the Cormorant de stated above, no outline of any other typeface is included.

Change log
----------
Regular: 1.000 (17.10.2026) first release; 1.001-1.016 spacing, kerning, outline polish, straight edges.
Italic: test builds I001-I025 (17-19.10.2026), see data/italic-version.json; not yet a final release.
Family: Regular and Italic linked as one family Very Peri Serif (19.10.2026).
